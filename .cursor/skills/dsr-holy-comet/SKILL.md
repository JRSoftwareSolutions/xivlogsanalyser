# Holy Comet

Follow `review-mechanic` for the packet, the outcome order, and a correction. Entry `holy-comet` in `fights/dsr/mechanics.json`. Guid `25577`. Part of Sanctity of the Ward (`dsr-sanctity-of-the-ward`). The session stop is Meteors. Holy Impact `25578` is the overlap.

Two prey players run and drop the comets. Prey belongs on north and south. The swap is in `dsr-sanctity-of-the-ward`.

## How to tell

Each drop is a light raidwide. A full resolve is about seven drops, roughly 45–55 hits across the party. Tanks take about 1.0–1.1k. Everyone else takes about 1.5–1.8k. Multiplier 1.00. This guid has not killed anyone. A few hits on pull 18 of `8DYNHQx4C7ytdLb9` reached about 6–7k and were still lived.

Comets that land too close are Holy Impact, not a large Holy Comet.

## Fault

A death on this small hit is the resolve. Name the shield, the mit, and the HP. Already under 15,000 HP is low.

Prey standing east or west is the prey player's mistake. The role partner who does not swap owns that mistake. Those positions are not on this damage packet.

Follow `analyze-mechanic-parameters`. Walked on `8DYNHQx4C7ytdLb9`. About 136–146 seconds. The whole party can be hit. No personal mitigation is required. The overlap is a different guid.
