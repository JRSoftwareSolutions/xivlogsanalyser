# Sanctity of the Ward

Follow `mechanic-components`. Draft from the FFXIV wiki's Dragonsong's Reprise page and from where the guids land in `XVz8bCqgPw1KRh9d`. The person confirms what belongs. Fault for a killing blow stays in that component's skill.

One knight sequence in two waves. It follows the Heavenly Heel tank swap. The person named Sanctity as the next mechanic. The session draws two stops. Sanctity of the Ward (`sanctity-of-the-ward`) is the first wave, about 112–119 seconds. Meteors (`meteors`) is the second wave, about 130–149 seconds. Both use this skill.

## First wave: gazes, jumps, dashes

| Component | Guid | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| the Dragon's Gaze | 25553, about 112–113s | Thordan's gaze. It resolves at the same time as Dragon's Glory. Looking at it deals damage and Hysteria. A successful gaze is 0. |
| the Dragon's Glory | 25554, about 112–113s | The eye's gaze, paired with Dragon's Gaze. Same result: damage and Hysteria if you look. A successful gaze is 0. Keep the two names apart when a death has only one of them. |
| Sacred Sever | 25571, about 112–119s | Ser Zephirin jumps on the player marked with one sword, then the player marked with two swords, then repeats. Each jump is a circle. Four players share it. The hit gets smaller as Zephirin jumps farther. A share of 1 or 2 under the role cap is missing bodies. A hit in the millions is the cleave, and that player was not in the stack. |
| Shining Blade | 25570, about 114–116s | Ser Adelphel and Ser Janlenoux dash and leave this cleave. This log has two hits, both kills. A hit is the failed dash. |
| Bright Flare | 25295, about 115–119s | The orbs those same dashes drop. One orb is about 64k. An overlap is over the role cap. Most pulls never store this guid, because a dodged orb deals nothing. |

## Second wave: ice, fire, towers, meteors

| Component | Guid | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hiemal Storm | 25575, about 130–132s | Ser Haumeric marks four players of one role with a circle. Each circle is shared by a pair and applies Ice Resistance Down II, so an overlap is lethal. It leaves an ice puddle. It does not split like a stack beyond that pair. |
| Frostbite | 1002946, about 133–137s | The damage from standing in a Hiemal Storm puddle. It is not a large Hiemal share. Any tick is a failed soak. |
| Heavens' Stake | 28591, about 130s | Ser Charibert's fire circles at the intercards and the fire donut on the edge. The puddles apply Burns. This log has three hits, all at this time. Any hit is standing in the fire. This is not the meteor. |
| Holy Comet | 25577, about 136–146s | Ser Noudenet marks two players of one role and drops seven meteors on them. Each drop is a light raidwide. This guid has not killed anyone in this log. Meteors that land too close become Holy Impact. |
| Holy Impact | 25578, about 141–149s | The wiki says this is the explosion when those meteors land too close, and that it wipes the party. The death rules currently call a hit inside the role cap raw, same shape as Eternal Conviction. Leave that call until the person picks one reading. |
| Conviction | 29564, about 134–136s | Ser Hermenost's towers. Eight hits on a long pull, one player each. An empty tower explodes for the raid. This guid is not in `mechanics.json` yet, so a death on it is still unknown until the person adds it. |
| Eternal Conviction | 25568, the hits around 130s | Same window as those towers. The earlier Eternal Conviction hits, around 63s, fall inside Strength of the Ward instead. The death rules treat a hit inside the role cap as raw and say it does not scale with how many people are hit. Whether the 130s hit is the soaked tower or the empty-tower raidwide is for the person to say. |

Faith Unmoving is the knockback in this wave on the wiki, and it is not a stored guid here. Ultimate End, Broad Swing, and Aetheric Burst come after Sanctity on the wiki. They are not components of this mechanic until the person adds them.
