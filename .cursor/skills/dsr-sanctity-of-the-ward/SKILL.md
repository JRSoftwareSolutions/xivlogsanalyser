# Sanctity of the Ward

Follow `mechanic-components`. The person described this sequence. Report `8DYNHQx4C7ytdLb9` is where the successful resolves were read. Fault for a killing blow stays in that component's skill.

One knight sequence in two waves. It follows Heavenly Heel, where the off tank takes the heel and the main tank takes the three cleaves. The session draws two stops. Sanctity of the Ward (`sanctity-of-the-ward`) is the first wave, about 112–119 seconds. Meteors (`meteors`) is the second wave, about 130–151 seconds. Both use this skill.

The two groups are the same two groups as the Lightning Storm triangles in Strength of the Ward.

## First wave: swords, gazes, dashes, jumps

Thordan disappears. A dark knight NPC appears offset from the center. Group 1 runs away from that NPC. Group 2 runs toward it.

Two players are marked, one sword or two swords.

- The single sword always runs away from the dark knight. Running toward it is that player's mistake.
- The double sword always runs toward the dark knight. Running away is that player's mistake.
- If the double sword is on someone from group 1, or the single sword is on someone from group 2, that player swaps with their role partner. Melees swap with melees, ranged with ranged, healers with healers, tanks with tanks.
- When the marked player runs the right way and the role partner does not swap, the partner owns that mistake.

## Second wave: fire, ice, towers, comets

Four fire circles spawn on the intercardinals. Thordan appears at the north edge.

Players preposition in support and DPS pairs. Melees with healers stand east and west. Tanks and ranged stand north and south. Two support players or two DPS players get a prey marker. Prey belongs on north and south.

- East swaps with north when the north player does not already have prey.
- West swaps with south when the south player does not already have prey.
- Prey still standing east or west is the prey player's mistake.
- The role partner who does not swap owns that mistake. That is the player without the prey marker.

### Towers

Each player has one quarter of the arena.

- Two towers outside that quarter: both players there move out.
- One tower outside: the player whose role was targeted by prey goes out. The other takes a center tower.
- Center towers are taken clockwise from that player. Taking a different tower is a mistake.
- If one tower is the next clockwise tower for two players, the player closer to it has it.
- When the towers resolve, everyone is inside one. Anyone outside made a mistake.
- After the comets, eight towers spawn outside. The role that had prey takes the cardinal towers. The other group takes the intercardinal towers. Anything else is a mistake.

Ultimate End comes after this mechanic. It is not a component until the person adds it.
