# Thordan opener

Follow `mechanic-components`. The person confirms this list. Fault for each hit stays in `dsr-ascalons-mercy-concealed` and `dsr-ascalons-might`.

The session draws two stops, in this order: Ascalon's Mercy Concealed (`ascalons-mercy-opener`), then Ascalon's Might (`ascalons-might-opener`). Both use this skill. The same two casts return later and are not these stops.

| Component | Guid | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ascalon's Mercy Concealed | 25545, about 14s | Narrow cones on every player. One tank stands in front. Everyone else stacks close behind the boss, then dodges. Nobody should be hit. A clean dodge deals no damage. A hit is that player's mistake. If someone baited a cone from outside the stack and the dodge then clips another player, the player who was out of the stack shares the blame, and the player who was hit still owns being in the cone. |
| Ascalon's Might | 25541, first cast, about 16s | Three cleaves on one tank. In this log the opener is the warrior. Correct play is an invuln or heavy personal mitigation. A second body, or anyone who is not a tank, owns that mistake. |

The three-hit at about 85s is Heavenly Heel (`dsr-heavenly-heel`), not this opener. The off tank has taken the heel. Those hits are properly mitigated, on the main tank. Sanctity of the Ward follows. The cones inside Strength of the Ward are that mechanic (`dsr-strength-of-the-ward`).
