# Faith Unmoving

Follow `review-mechanic` for the packet, the outcome order, and a correction. Entry `faith-unmoving` in `fights/dsr/mechanics.json`. Guid `25308`. Part of Sanctity of the Ward (`dsr-sanctity-of-the-ward`). The session stop is Meteors.

The knockback happens just before the eight outer towers resolve. Players in the middle are knocked into their tower. Players already outside use knockback immunity so they are not knocked into the wall.

## How to tell

A correct knockback still deals damage. Tanks take about 2.7–3.1k. Everyone else takes about 4.5–5.2k. A few hits sit under 1.5k when mitigation is heavy. Multiplier is usually 1.00. Clean pulls that reach the outer towers hit the party. This guid has not killed anyone in either log.

Being knocked into the wall is a mistake. These logs do not store a separate wall hit. Do not invent one.

## Fault

A death on this small hit is the resolve, or low if they were already under 15,000 HP. The wall is a mistake when a later review can see it. This packet does not show the wall.

## Parameters

Follow `analyze-mechanic-parameters`. Walked on `8DYNHQx4C7ytdLb9`. About 145–146 seconds. The party is hit. No personal mitigation is required for the players who take the knockback into a tower. The outside players' immunity is the plan. A missing hit is not, by itself, a missed immunity. Pull 27 stored six hits and still had no wall death on this guid.
