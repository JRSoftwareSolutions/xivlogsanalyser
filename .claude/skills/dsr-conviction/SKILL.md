# Conviction

Follow `review-mechanic` for the packet, the outcome order, and a correction. Entry `conviction` in `fights/dsr/mechanics.json`. Guids `29564` and `28651`. Part of Sanctity of the Ward (`dsr-sanctity-of-the-ward`). The session stop is Meteors.

`29564` resolves about 135 seconds. Those towers are inside and outside the arena. `28651` resolves about 148 seconds. Those eight towers are on the outside, after the comets. Eternal Conviction `25568` is the empty tower. Faith Unmoving `25308` is the knockback before the outer towers.

## How to tell

A soaked tower is one player. Tanks take about 2.9–3.4k. Everyone else takes about 4.6–5.4k. Multiplier 1.00. A clean pull has eight of these hits. Pull 27 of `8DYNHQx4C7ytdLb9` has two lived hits around 10k on `28651`. They stayed inside the cap.

This guid has not killed anyone in either log. A death on the soak is the small hit. An empty tower is not this guid.

## Fault

The plan is in `dsr-sanctity-of-the-ward`. Each player owns one quarter. Two outside towers means both move out. One outside tower means the prey role moves out and the partner takes the center tower clockwise, with the closer player taking a tower that is next for two people. After the comets, the prey role takes cardinal towers and the other group takes intercardinal towers. The wrong tower is that player's mistake. Missing a tower is a mistake, and the explosion is judged on Eternal Conviction.
